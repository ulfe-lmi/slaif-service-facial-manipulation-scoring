# Third-party data notice

This directory contains aggregate evaluation results, derived plot points/bins, and limited score rows. It contains no benchmark image.

Identified score rows are included only for FFHQ and FFHQR and remain subject to the respective dataset and source-image terms, including non-commercial/share-alike conditions where applicable. They are not covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 code license.

STR-FFHQ-FM, READFake, CelebHQ-FM, PPR10K, MFFI, and RetouchingFFHQ are represented by aggregate evidence only because their redistribution terms are restrictive or insufficiently explicit for identified rows or images.

Consult [`../docs/datasets.md`](../docs/datasets.md) before reusing any evidence or mapping a score identifier back to an image.
